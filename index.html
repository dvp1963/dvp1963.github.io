What it does

  • No-deco planner: NDL for air and nitrox, safety stop, repetitive dives, altitude and fresh water.
  • Technical planner: multi-level profiles, any gases, automatic deco-gas switches by ppO₂, runtime table, ceiling chart, tissue loading, CNS/OTU, gas consumption, model comparison, contingency grid.
  • Rebreather: setpoints, diluent and up to four bailout cylinders, bailout schedule, bailout advisor by depth.
  • Logbook: manual entries from a plan, Garmin Connect import, suit type, thickness and weights kept with every plan and dive.
  • Certifications: your cards with private JPG/PNG/PDF scans stored only on the device.

Important. DecoPlan is a planning tool for trained divers. Every decompression model is an approximation: verify plans with another planner and your dive computer, keep gas reserves and never dive beyond your training.
